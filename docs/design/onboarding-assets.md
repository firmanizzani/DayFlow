## Final prompt set

Shared direction: a premium Dayflow onboarding illustration, isolated on a genuinely transparent background; soft 3D editorial objects with rounded matte paper/ceramic materials, gentle bevels, subtle ambient occlusion and short soft contact shadows. Lavender #A99BFF, purple #7366F0, deep indigo #393276, warm-white paper, mint #DDF8EE, and peach #FFE9DE. Three-quarter front orthographic view, upper-left daylight, one dominant object with transparent margins and a readable silhouette at 250px. No text, numerals, logos, watermarks, humans, character faces, confetti, arbitrary decorative spheres/blobs, environment or background plane. Works on both lavender and dark-navy app backgrounds.

Capture subject: a slightly open warm-white checklist notebook with a lavender hard cover, thick curved spine, three tactile indigo checklist squares, embossed lavender lines, one mint check mark, a purple pencil and a peach page tab. The notebook is the hero and conveys capturing and organizing a task.

Plan subject: a rounded lavender desk calendar with two indigo ring binders, a warm-white page, an unnumbered indented calendar grid, one selected purple date tile, mint-and-white analog clock without numerals, and two restrained peach/lavender time-block tabs. The calendar is the hero and conveys giving a task a place in the day.

Focus subject: a rounded lavender focus timer with an indigo face, matte mint progress arc, warm-white analog hands without numbers, one warm-white task card with a mint check mark and embossed lavender lines, and an integrated peach pause button. The timer is the hero and conveys concentration and a small completed step.

The requested square canvas was 1024×1024; each delivered PNG is 1254×1254 with an alpha channel. Original files remain in the generation output folder; project copies are the source assets used by the mobile bundle. No generated screenshot is used as an interactive interface.
